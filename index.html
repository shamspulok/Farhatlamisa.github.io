Focus Areas

Research Interests



My research lies at the intersection of environmental toxicology, contaminant fate and transport, environmental risk analysis, and ecosystem sustainability. Through the integration of environmental monitoring, spatial analysis, and geospatial modelling, I investigate the movement, transformation, and impacts of hazardous chemicals and emerging contaminants across environmental systems. My work is further driven by interests in pollution mitigation, hazardous waste treatment, waste remediation, and sustainable environmental management, supported by a strong foundation in chemistry, including organic and polymer synthesis.
